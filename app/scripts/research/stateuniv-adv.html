Procurement Notices can be in one of four statuses.

  • Published - Notice is a current procurement opportunity for vendors.
  • Awarded - A procurement opportunity that has been awarded.
  • Cancelled - A procurement opportunity that has been cancelled prior to the opening date.
  • Non-Awarded - A procurement opportunity that was determined after the opening date to not be awarded.

Procurement notices can be either Competitive or Non-Competitive.

Competitive Notices are an open and competitive procurement opportunity for vendors. Examples include, but are not limited to IFB, RFP, QBS, RFI-RP, and PA-RFP.

Non-Competitive notices are for publishing an intent to award based on a specific procurement method. Examples include, but are not limited to Sole Source, Emergency, General Award, and Exemption.

More specific information on the notice types can be found in the Glossary.
